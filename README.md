# Contrastive Speech Analytics & Temporal Flaw Grounding
_Multimodal AI Hackathon 2026 — Track C. Working name: TBD._

A speech-delivery debugging system. It builds controlled contrastive pairs of
"ideal" vs "flawed" speech, locates the flaw in time **blind** (no answer key), explains it
from **measured acoustic evidence** (not LLM guesses), and reports honest held-out metrics.

> Status: scaffolding only (Session 0). No working pipeline yet. See `docs/SESSION_LOG.md`.

## Why it's not just "ask an LLM"
- Same transcript, controlled delivery edits with logged parameters → known ground-truth intervals.
- Detector sees audio only, predicts the flawed interval, then we reveal the label and measure error.
- Every explanation is generated from a structured evidence object; the LLM may phrase it but
  cannot invent a number, timestamp, score, or label.
